❓ How to Use This Tool

A quick walkthrough before you start your first Self-Assessment.
📹 Video walkthrough coming soon — for now, follow the steps below.
🗓️ You do NOT have to finish this in one sitting. You have the entire quarter to complete it. Answer a few questions, tap "💾 Save Now" (or just close the app — it saves automatically), and come back whenever you have time. Nothing is lost.
🔘 What Each Button Does, In Order
💾
Save Now — saves your progress right now. You don't need this often (it auto-saves as you go), but it's there for peace of mind.
📊
Summary — shows your current score and a list of everything you've flagged so far. Check this any time.
📋
Action List — every NO you've answered, in one place, with a status you can update (Open / In Progress / Resolved) as you work through the quarter — even weeks after you finish the assessment itself.
✅
Finalize — only tap this once you've answered every question and are truly done. It locks your answers in. (You can still unlock it later if you made a mistake.)
⬇
Excel (not Print/PDF) — tap this after finalizing to download your results as a file. This step comes before Send Audit.
✉️
Send Audit — tap this last. It opens an email pre-filled with the right recipients — just attach the file you just downloaded and hit send.
🔄
New Audit — this is also how you delete/reset the current audit. Use it for starting a genuinely new assessment (like next quarter), or to wipe a mistake and start completely over. Don't tap this if you're just taking a break — your progress is already saved automatically.
📋 The Full Process, Step by Step
1
Pick your location. Tap the search box on the first screen and type your location's name — select it from the list that appears.
2
Enter your name and today's date. The quarter fills in on its own — no need to figure that out yourself.
3
Check your sections are right. Most sections are already turned on for you. If your location has vehicles or golf carts, make sure those are switched on too.
4
Go through each question, whenever you have time. Tap YES, NO, or N/A for every item. If you tap NO, add a short note about what's wrong. If you tap N/A, add a short reason why it doesn't apply. Stop and come back as many times as you need.
5
Check your Action List as you go. Update item statuses throughout the quarter as things get fixed — this doesn't require finishing the assessment first.
6
Tap Finalize once every question is answered. Made a mistake after finalizing? Tap "Unlock to Fix a Mistake" — you can always go back in.
7
Tap Excel, then Send Audit. Attach the downloaded file to the email that opens, and hit send.
💡 A Few Extra Tips
💡 Never use your browser's Print or Save as PDF button. Always use the green Excel button — that's the only format that can be tracked properly.
💡 Your progress saves automatically as you go. It's safe to close the app and come back later — you'll pick up right where you left off.
📱 iPhone users: the Send Audit button may open Apple's built-in Mail app instead of Outlook. To fix this permanently: go to your iPhone's Settings → Outlook → Default Mail App and select Outlook. This is a one-time fix for your whole phone, not just this app.
🗑️ Need to Delete or Reset an Audit?
1
If you're already inside the audit, tap the orange "🔄 New Audit" button. If you're on the setup screen with a saved audit showing, tap "🗑️ Delete Saved" right next to your location name instead — same result, either way.
2
The button changes to "⚠️ Tap again — [X] lost", showing exactly how many answers will be deleted. This is your warning — tap anything else to cancel, or wait a few seconds and it resets on its own.
3
Tap the same button again to confirm. Every answer, note, and Action List entry for the current audit is cleared.
⚠️ This cannot be undone. If you might need what you've entered, tap Excel to save a copy first before deleting.
🔄 App Acting Strange or Out of Date?
Tap the "🔄 Refresh" button near the top of the screen. This is safe — your progress is saved automatically before it refreshes, so you won't lose anything. Use this any time something looks wrong, missing, or like an older version of the app.
📝 No internet or having trouble with the app?
There's a paper-free backup option: a fillable Excel version of this same audit. Ask your Risk VP for the file — pick your location from the dropdown at the top and everything else works the same way, just typed in directly instead of tapped.
